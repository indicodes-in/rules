# Code of conduct

This repository is about getting public rules right. Keep discussion to that.

- Argue from the source. Cite the page; disagree with the reading, not the person.
- Be patient with people new to the documents or to Git — both are hard.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, here or in private messages
  about work here.
- Don't post anyone's personal details, or details of private plots, owners
  or applications. Benchmark cases are anonymised; keep them that way.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who don't follow
this. Report problems by opening an issue marked *conduct*, or privately to
the maintainers through www.indicodes.com.
